# Introduction
This post records the fishing tackle I use in 2026, mainly for my own reference. It reflects my setup as of November 2026, and the specs come from the makers' published figures.

*Note: Amazon links in this post include affiliate links (PR).*

I started fishing in earnest this year. Before that, I only did casual sabiki and bait fishing.

When I started, I wanted fishing that met these conditions:

- Plenty of bites and a good chance of a catch, even if the fish run small
- Fish that taste good
- Light, simple gear

So I began with light game (light rockfish and aji, or horse mackerel). It soon felt too tame, and I took up hard rockfish fishing as well.

Two principles run through my setup:

- I keep worms to three types that give me options for most situations.
- I choose hooks for catching many fish rather than for landing big ones.
