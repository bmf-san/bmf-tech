A reading list for engineers who spend long hours at a desk and want to stay healthy. Each pick has its own review on this blog, and every entry links to it. The list starts with health hacks for engineers, then moves through improving habits, living with stress, and evidence-based health.

## Why engineers should learn about health

Engineering work keeps you seated, so posture, lack of exercise, and stress take a toll. To keep working for years, you need to know how to look after your body and mind, just as you keep learning technology. Health builds up from daily habits, so the earlier you learn, the more it pays off.

## 1. Health for programmers

### The Healthy Programmer

It introduces health hacks, centered on exercise, that help programmers keep enjoying their work. It makes you realize how much it matters to change posture and move often instead of holding one position. I cover it in the [review](/posts/healthy-programmer-hacks/).

### "Agile" Health Improvement Guide

An unusual book that applies agile thinking to improving your health. It spells out concrete habits and behavior patterns, so you can put it into practice. See the [review](/posts/agile-health-improvement-guide/).

## 2. Live with stress

### Stress-Free Mastery

A book on how to face stress. You learn mindsets, ways of thinking, and actions that help you get along with stress. I wrote it up in the [review](/posts/stress-free-guide/).

## 3. Think about health with evidence

### The Complete Guide to Health Technology

A health book that leans hard on evidence, with an overwhelming list of references. It gives diet the most pages and teaches you to judge food by higher or lower risk rather than good or bad. I cover it in the [review](/posts/health-technology-guide/).

## Wrap-up

Start with The Healthy Programmer to learn the risks of desk work and what to do about them, then improve small habits with the "Agile" Health Improvement Guide. When you want to dig into stress or diet, the other two books help. Every book has a full review here, so start with whichever one interests you most.
